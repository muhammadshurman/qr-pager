QRPAGER-15 §7: "Three consecutive green full-suite runs, transcripts committed."

Produced by tools/gate/bin/three-green-runs, the sibling of apps/api/bin/three-green-runs
and committed for the reason that script's header gives: Task 4 met the same bar by running
its gate three times by hand and writing the results into a file, and R-043 is the §5 row
about exactly that shape — a gate clause whose demonstration lives in a session rather than
in the repository, so nobody can re-run it.

WHAT "CONSECUTIVE" MEANS HERE: one commit, no edits between runs, and a red STOPS the loop
rather than being retried until it passes. Three greens out of five attempts is not three
consecutive greens, and the difference is the whole point.

The script also REFUSES to stay quiet about a dirty tree, and that fired on the first
attempt: three runs at a commit mean nothing if the tree changes under them. The run below
is on a clean tree.

WHAT IT RUNS is the whole workspace gate — the Vitest projects, the Playwright browser
suite AND both Next builds. A "full suite" that skipped the browser checks would skip the
four R-118 clauses this task closes, which are the ones that cannot be asserted anywhere
else.

The PHP half has its own three-green-runs and its own known intermittent red (R-092);
mixing them would let one half's flake stop the other's proof.

--- reading ---
Every cell resolves to rgb(11, 107, 79) on rgb(255, 255, 255) for the primary fill and
rgb(150, 53, 26) on rgb(255, 255, 255) for the danger fill, with appearance=auto giving
the same values as appearance=none, under both load sequences. NO BLEND APPEARS.

SO THE CLAIM IS CORRECTED IN PLACE rather than defended. Two readings are consistent
with everything else in the record and this transcript cannot separate them:

  (a) the blend was the LOAD-THEN-INJECT harness defect and was attributed to
      'appearance' by mistake. PLAN §4a records the harness defect separately, with the
      measured pair rgb(174,201,193) / rgb(73,73,73) 'varying in its last bit between
      runs' -- which is within one unit of the rgb(174,202,194) this claim names. Two
      defects found in the same sitting, one cause written against both.

  (b) something about the original page -- the full kit stylesheet, a filter, an
      opacity, a transition mid-flight -- participated, and the minimal probe above
      does not carry it.

WHAT IS NOT IN DOUBT, AND IS THE LIVE CONTROL: browser/a11y.spec.ts asserts the ENGINE
resolves every button to the EXACT pair the contrast table was computed from, with three
proofs that the assertion fails (stylesheet withheld, wrong theme, the historical
sequence pinned as an invariant). Any blend, whatever its cause, reddens that. The
finding that matters survives; its stated CAUSE did not, and 'appearance: none' stays
because normalising a native widget's styling is correct regardless of this question.

The probe is a transcript rather than a committed test, deliberately: an assertion that
Chromium does NOT blend is a test of the browser, and the exact-pair assertion above is
the test of our code. The spec file that produced this is in the commit message.
